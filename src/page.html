How to play

Every board is a blacked-out 30 by 30 city. One beam leaves the arrow and runs until something stops it. Bend it through as much value as you can.

  • Circles add their number, diamonds subtract theirs. Each scores once.
  • Mirrors turn the beam 90 degrees and work from both sides. You get 12.
  • Splitters let the beam carry straight on and also send a copy sideways. You get 2.
  • Blockades stop the beam. Pieces can't sit on blockades, targets or the start.
  • Your best score saves itself every time you beat it. When the clock stops, your best layout is the one that counts, so experimenting costs nothing.
  • Save a layout and start over as many times as you like. Select a saved layout to bring it back.

Click a cell on the beam to place the selected piece. Hovering first shows the new route and the change in score. Click a piece to flip it, drag it to move it, right-click to remove it.

The same board code always gives the same city, so you can compare scores on any board.
